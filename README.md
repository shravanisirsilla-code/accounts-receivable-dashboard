# Accounts Receivable & Invoice Tracking System

## Project Overview
An Excel-based Accounts Receivable tracking and analysis project developed to monitor customer invoices, payment status, outstanding balances and receivables ageing.

The project demonstrates the use of Microsoft Excel for organising invoice records, analysing collections and monitoring overdue receivables.

## Project Objectives
- Track customer invoices and payment details.
- Calculate outstanding balances.
- Monitor paid, partially paid and unpaid invoices.
- Categorise receivables based on ageing.
- Analyse customer-wise outstanding balances.
- Monitor invoice and payment trends.
- Present financial metrics through a dashboard.

## Dataset
- Total records: 100 fictional invoices.
- Data fields: Invoice ID, Customer Name, Invoice Date, Due Date, Invoice Amount, Amount Paid and Payment Date.
- Reporting date: 31 October 2026.
